MISSION STATEMENT & CALIBRATION DIRECTIVE

The Aikya Apex Initiative addresses the fundamental sensor transition discontinuity in global wildland fire monitoring. For over two decades, NASA’s MODIS instruments aboard Terra and Aqua established baseline measurements of fire occurrence, radiative power, and diurnal phenology. In 2012, the VIIRS instrument introduced 375m sub-pixel thermal sensitivity, detecting roughly 3.2 times more active fire pixels due to its sharper point spread function and constrained limb-viewing footprint aggregation.

Without rigorous cross-sensor calibration, longitudinal climate records exhibit an artificial step-change jump that misleads emergency responders, climate modelers, and public land managers. Aikya Apex applies power-law cluster aggregation, Spectral Band Adjustment Factors (SBAF), and RossThick-LiSparse BRDF directional corrections to reconcile multi-decadal fire records into an authoritative, single-truth historical record.
